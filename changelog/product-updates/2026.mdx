<Update label="November 26 2026" description="Turn points into wallet balance on Shopify & Salla">

## New Features

### [Customers Can Turn Their Points into Money They Can Spend](/product-documentation/programs/loyalty-points-redeem/launch-and-configure-your-redeem-pointing-system)

Customers can now convert their points into wallet balance: real money added to their account in your store, ready to spend at checkout. It is more flexible than a coupon, and one deposit can be spent across one or more orders. On Shopify it is called store credit, and on Salla it is the customer wallet. Available on all plans.

* Recover points before they expire by encouraging customers to turn them into wallet balance
* Add a wallet balance redemption option and set how many points buy how much balance, for example 500 points = 25 USD
* Customers redeem from the widget, and the balance is added right away with no code to copy
* On Salla, give wallet balance as a reward for campaigns (including welcome and birthday), tier upgrades and referrals, with a custom expiry of up to 5 years
* If your store is not set up yet, the forms show what is missing and link you to the setting

</Update>

<Update label="November 24 2026" description="Spin & Win start page & every setting on the details page">

## New Features

### [Shape Spin & Win from the First Screen to the Win Screen](/product-documentation/rewards-campaigns/templates/engagement-gamification/spin-the-wheel)

Spin & Win campaigns can now welcome customers with a start page before the wheel, and you decide how the win screen looks and whether customers can spin again. Game lead forms also get a new field type: yes / no checkboxes, in every game that has a lead form. Available on the same plans as your game campaigns.

* Turn on a start page from the new **Intro** tab in **Customize design**, with a title, a description, a button and an optional image in each campaign language
* Customers see the start page when they open the wheel from a link, its card or the games banner, while popups and calendar days open straight on the wheel
* Add a heading above the win message, in **Normal** or **Large** size
* Choose whether **Spin again** shows on the win and No luck screens, so you can keep it to one spin
* Add yes / no customer attributes to any game's lead form: customers see a checkbox, and ticking it sets the attribute on their profile
* Existing campaigns stay as they are: no start page, no heading, and Spin again still shown until you change them

***

## Other Improvements

### See Every Campaign Setting on Its Details Page

Settings you make on a campaign's inner pages, such as each prize's winning chance, reward limit and daily supply cap, now show on the campaign's details page, so you can check what a campaign does without opening the editor. The same settings also show on the edit-page card that opens each inner page. Available on all plans.

</Update>

<Update label="November 23 2026" description="App Popup games, customer notes & push tracking">

## New Features

### [Launch Game Campaigns as a Popup in Your Mobile App](/product-documentation/rewards-campaigns/launch-game-campaign-app-popup)

Game campaigns can now open by themselves inside your mobile app, so customers reach a game without opening the widget first. Set up the in-app integration once, then launch any game campaign as an App Popup from the dashboard, with almost no custom development per campaign. App Popup isn't available for campaigns set to Web only. Available on all plans.

* Pick **App Popup** under **Rules → Triggers**, next to Widget banner and Web Popup
* Launch any of the 11 game campaigns this way, from Spin the Wheel to Driving Game
* Open the popup when the app opens, or when a custom event happens, with up to 10 conditions on the event's properties
* Show it as a Fullscreen or Modal popup
* Control how often it appears with the same rules as in-app messages: the repeat setting, minimum interval, cooldown and quiet hours

***

### [Leave Internal Notes on a Customer](/product-documentation/understanding-your-customers/customer-profile/explore-and-manage-a-customer-profile)

A new **Notes** card on the customer profile gives your team a place to keep context on a customer, so the next teammate who opens the profile doesn't start from zero. Notes are for your team only and are never shown to the customer. Available on all plans.

* Add a note of up to 1,000 characters from the Notes card on the Overview tab
* See every note newest first, with who wrote it and when, and hover the date for the exact time
* Delete a note you wrote; only its author can delete it

***

### [Track Every Push: Delivered, Clicked, Dismissed and Influenced Opens](/product-documentation/communication-campaigns/create-and-configure-mobile-push-campaigns#push-delivery-and-engagement-metrics)

Gameball now tracks what happens after you send a push: whether it was delivered, whether customers clicked or dismissed it, and whether it led to an app open. Delivery is tracked by Gameball, while clicks and dismissals are reported by the Gameball mobile SDK, so those numbers start filling in once your app is on the updated SDK. Available on all plans.

* **Delivered:** the share of pushes that went through out of all the pushes attempted. Temporary provider errors aren't counted as failures
* **Clicked:** customers who tapped your push
* **Dismissed:** customers who dismissed your push
* **Influenced opens:** app opens that followed your push, even when the customer didn't tap it

***

## Other Improvements

### [See Held Points and the Next Points to Expire](/product-documentation/understanding-your-customers/customer-profile/explore-and-manage-a-customer-profile)

A customer's **Points Balance** now shows their **On-hold** points, with a **Show release date** button that tells you when each hold returns to the balance, and the next points due to expire and when. Your team can now explain a customer's balance without opening a support ticket.

</Update>

<Update label="November 19 2026" description="A faster, fairer leaderboard with Normal and Advanced tiers">

## New Features

### [A Leaderboard That Loads Instantly, Ranks on What Customers Kept, and Comes in Two Tiers](/product-documentation/customer-widget/start-configuring-profile/control-gameball-leaderboard-on-widget)

The leaderboard runs on a new engine, so it loads in under a second in your widget instead of keeping customers waiting. Refunds and cancellations now come off a customer's score in the period of the original purchase, so rankings reflect what customers kept. The leaderboard comes in two tiers: **Normal**, included with every plan that has the widget leaderboard, and **Advanced**, which adds more periods, deeper rankings, and the Leaderboard API. Every merchant using the leaderboard today moves to Advanced, so nobody loses anything. Available on Shopify Pro and Guru, Salla Guru, and Enterprise self-serve plans (add-on on Growth). Advanced needs the **Advanced leaderboard** plan feature and an active subscription.

* Show customers a leaderboard that opens in under a second, down from around 16 seconds
* Rank customers on net totals: refunds and cancellations count against the original purchase period, and fraud-blocked transactions never count
* Rank by your levels method (amount spent, points, orders, referrals, or score), with ties always shown in the same order
* On Advanced, add weekly and daily leaderboards, rank up to 10,000 customers, and show every customer their exact rank
* On Advanced, pull rankings from the [Leaderboard API](/api-reference/leaderboard/customer-leaderboard) with custom date ranges and filters by customer tag, reward campaign, or campaign tag

</Update>

<Update label="November 18 2026" description="Push A/B Testing & Messaging Performance">

## New Features

### [A/B Test Your Push Notifications](/product-documentation/communication-campaigns/create-and-configure-mobile-push-campaigns)

Write several versions of the same push, split your audience between them, and keep a control group that gets nothing. After launch, the campaign page shows which version drove more conversions and how much better it did than the control group. Available on all plans.

* Create two or more variants, each with its own text, image, tap action, and buttons
* Split your audience by percentage, with every customer always getting the same variant
* Optional control group that receives no push, so you can measure the real uplift
* Compare customers, sent, conversions, conversion rate, and uplift in a table and a chart
* Variants and the split are locked once the campaign is live

***

### [Messaging Performance](/product-documentation/analytics/messaging-performance)

See how all your messaging performs in one place. The new Messaging Performance page under Analytics brings your outbound results together, so you can compare channels and campaigns without opening each campaign. Available on all plans.

* Switch between Email, Push, Web, SMS, WhatsApp, and In-app
* See the main numbers for a channel at a glance, with charts over time
* Compare up to 10 campaigns by sends, impressions, reached customers, and conversion rate, plus clicks and click rate for Email, Web, and In-app
* Find it under Analytics → Messaging Performance, for team members who can view both Analytics and Communication

***

### [Tap Actions, Buttons, and Images for Push Notifications](/product-documentation/communication-campaigns/create-and-configure-mobile-push-campaigns)

Push campaigns now control what a push does, not just what it says. Campaigns that don't use the new settings send exactly the same push as before. Available on all plans.

* Choose what a tap does: open the app, open a screen, or open a web page, inside the app if you prefer
* Add up to 3 buttons, each with a label per campaign language and its own tap action
* Attach an image to the push (Android only for now)
* Set an expiry of up to 28 days
* Target iOS, Android, or both, and send up to 20 custom data pairs to your app

***

### [Send In-App Step in Automations](/product-documentation/automation-campaigns/automation-actions)

Automations can now reach customers inside your app. Add the new Send In-App step and customers who reach it see your in-app message on their next session, once. Available on all plans.

* Add a Send In-App step to an automation and save it
* Each customer sees the message once, not every session

</Update>

<Update label="November 17 2026" description="Richer customer webhook & cashback API">

## New Features

### Customer Webhook: Status, Deletion and Referral Data

The customer update webhook now tells you whether a customer is part of your loyalty program, who referred them, and how many customers they have referred. When a customer is deleted, you receive a final webhook that says so, so your own systems stay in sync without extra API calls. Available on all plans.

* See whether each customer is included in or excluded from your loyalty program
* Get notified when a customer is deleted, with a clear deleted flag on the final update
* See who referred a customer and whether their referral is activated
* Track each customer's total, active, and pending referrals
* Existing fields don't change. The new fields are added on webhook version 4

[Learn more](/api-reference/webhooks/webhook-topics/profile-updates)

## Other Improvements

### Cashback Settings API Returns Full Earning Rules

The Cashback Settings API now returns your default rule's eligibility (which products and collections it covers), your custom cashback rules, and your points multiplier campaigns. Developers can work out what an order will earn before checkout. Available on all plans. [Learn more](/api-reference/configurations/reward-configurations/cashback-settings)

***

### A Complete Guide to Your Data Warehouse Tables

The [Data Warehouse article](/product-documentation/admin-settings/data-warehouse-ingestion) now covers all 6 shared tables and shows how to rebuild your dashboard numbers from them: points earned, redeemed, expired and expiring soon, outstanding balance, sources, and coupons. Open it from the new **Learn more** link on **Settings → Data warehouse integration**. Available on all plans (add-on on Shopify and Salla).

</Update>

<Update label="November 4 2026" description="In-App Messaging, Multi-Currency, Redemption Groups, Game Improvements & More">

## New Features

### In-App Messaging for Mobile Apps

Reach customers while they're already in your app. In-App Messaging is a new channel in Communication Campaigns — build announcements in the dashboard and show them inside your iOS and Android app at the right moment, with no app update needed.

* Three formats: slide-up banner, modal card, and full screen
* Trigger on session start or on any custom event, with property filters
* Frequency caps, quiet hours, priority, and expiry per campaign
* Impressions, clicks, and dismissals tracked automatically
* Available as an opt-in module in iOS, Android, Flutter, and React Native SDKs (4.0.0)

***

### Multi-Currency Support

Show loyalty values in each shopper's own currency. Set a base currency, add currencies with your own exchange rates, and Gameball handles conversion on orders, in the widget, and across the API. All rules, analytics, and liability stay in your base currency.

* Manual exchange rates, editable at any time
* Widget and mobile SDKs show points value in the shopper's currency
* New "Last order currency" and "Current currency" customer attributes for targeting
* Salla stores follow the currency the shopper picked on the storefront

***

### Dashboard Dark Mode

Switch the whole dashboard to a dark theme from the sun/moon toggle in the top bar.

* Covers navigation, home, campaigns, tables, forms, and analytics
* Your preference is remembered across sessions

***

### Redemption Groups

Organise your rewards into named groups — such as "Coffee", "Merch", or "Experiences" — so customers can filter by group in the widget's Redeem tab.

* Create, rename, reorder, and delete groups from the new Manage Groups drawer
* Assign options in bulk or per option
* Merchants without groups get automatic chips by reward type instead

***

### Points Analytics per Branch

Analytics > Points & Rewards now breaks down points activity per branch, grouped under its merchant.

* One row per branch: Earned, Refunded, Net Earned, Redeemed, Redeemed in Branch, Expired, and Current Liability
* Expand a branch to see each column as a percentage
* A "No branch" row covers widget redemptions, manual adjustments, and orders sent without a branch

***

### Per-Reward Notifications in Game Campaigns

Spin & Win, Slot Machine, Scratch & Win, and Match Cards can now send a different notification for each reward and for No Luck, instead of one generic message per campaign.

* Choose Global or Per Reward Notification separately for in-app and email
* Build a dedicated email per reward using the Email Builder
* The campaign overview warns you when only some rewards have an email

***

### Reward Supply Cap & Accurate Game Odds

Each game reward can now have a supply cap — how many times it can be won across all customers per day, week, or month. When a reward runs out, the odds stay the same and plays that land on it show No Luck instead.

* Optional daily, weekly, or monthly cap per reward
* Works alongside the existing per-customer Reward Limit
* No Luck reward added automatically when any cap is set
* Customers never see that a reward is capped

***

### Refer Existing Customers

Bring existing customers — such as in-store shoppers — into your digital loyalty program through a referral. Customers already in Gameball can now be referred when they join your website or app, via a referral link or a customer update with a referral code.

* Works regardless of account age, migration status, or past orders
* Friend reward given on linking; referrer rewarded on the friend's first qualifying action after linking
* Orders and actions before linking do not count toward the referrer reward

***

## Other Improvements

### Game Play Limits: Overall and per Period

Game campaigns can now cap plays per customer both overall and per day, week, or month. Raising a limit on a live campaign makes customers eligible again immediately.

***

### More Operators for Order Properties

Order property conditions now support the full operator set: "is not", "greater than", "less than", and "less than or equal" for numbers; "is not", "does not contain", "starts with", "ends with", "is empty", and "is not empty" for text. Available in earn rules, referrals, segments, reward campaigns, communication campaigns, and automations.

***

### See Who Created Each Coupon

The Coupons page now records and shows who created every coupon — a team member's email, "API", or "Gameball support". A new Created by column, filter, and export column make it easy to trace any coupon back to its source.

***

### Locked Coupons Show as Unavailable

Coupons locked against an active booking are now clearly unavailable everywhere: greyed out in the widget, flagged in the Get Customer Coupons API, and shown with a Locked indicator in the customer profile.

***

### New Customer Tags Drawer

Adding, removing, and creating customer tags now happens in one consistent drawer from both the Customers list and individual profiles. Create a new tag inline without opening a second window.

***

### Coupon Eligibility Rules

The coupon "Applies To" section now includes a rule builder so you can make a coupon available only when the order qualifies — using the same order attributes as reward campaigns.

## New Features

### Streak Protection: Restore or Freeze

Check-in Streak campaigns can now include streak protection, so customers who have built a long streak can restore it after a break or freeze it before it breaks. One missed day no longer pushes your most engaged customers out of the campaign. Available on Pro and Guru plans.

* All protection copy on the widget is fully editable

***

### Completion Window for Missions & Stamps

Give each customer a fixed number of days to finish a Mission or Stamps campaign once they start. The customer's clock runs separately from the campaign schedule, so a month-long campaign can still ask each customer to finish within 5 days. Available on Pro and Guru plans.

* Works with sequential and non-sequential Missions, and with Stamps campaigns
* Customers see their remaining days on the mission or stamps card
* Countdown wording is editable per language in the Customization step

### WhatsApp Messages in Automations

Add a **Send WhatsApp** step to any automation flow and reach customers on WhatsApp at the right moment in their journey. Available to accounts with the WhatsApp channel enabled.

* Sent, delivered, and failed counts show on the node

***

### Real-time Segmentation

Segments now update in real time. When a customer places an order, earns or redeems points, changes tier, or updates their profile, they move in or out of the right segments within seconds.

* Segments that rely on order or event history keep refreshing on a schedule
* Every segment shows whether it refreshes instantly or on a schedule

***

### Points Receipt for Every Order

The order details drawer now shows exactly why an order earned the points it did, from each item's eligibility to the rate applied and any campaign boosts.

* Order summary with points earned, value, tier, balance before, and expiry

* Items that earned no points show a clear reason

### Campaign Points Lifecycle Breakdown

### Return Coupons on Full Refund
