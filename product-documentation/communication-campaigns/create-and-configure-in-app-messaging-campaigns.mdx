Your customers open your app every day. With **Gameball's In-App Messages**, you can talk to them right there. Show a slide-up, a modal, or a full-screen message inside your iOS and Android apps, at the moment that matters. You build and change every message from the Gameball dashboard, so there is no app update to ship.

---

## Understanding In-App Messages

In-app messages appear while the customer is already using your app. Push notifications try to bring customers back. In-app messages guide them while they are already there.

Only customers who open your app can see an in-app message. Nothing is sent by push, email, or SMS.

### Message Types

Message types are ordered from least to most interruptive.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Slide-up" icon="bars-progress">
    A small banner that slides in. The app stays usable underneath, so it is the least disruptive type. Best for nudges and reminders.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Modal" icon="window-maximize">
    A centered card over dimmed content. Best for offers and moments that need a decision.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Full Screen" icon="mobile-screen">
    Takes the whole screen. Best for big announcements and welcome moments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Ideas to Get Started

Here are some campaigns loyalty teams run with in-app messages:

| Idea | Audience | Trigger | Message type | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Checkout upsell.** "Add a dessert and earn 50 bonus points." | All customers | Action based: your app's cart viewed event | Slide-up | None |
| **Points about to expire.** Show what the points are worth, with a button to redeem them. | Segment: points expiring soon | Session start | Modal | Points redeemed |
| **Profile completion.** Offer 200 points to finish the profile. | New members | Session start, once per customer | Full screen | Profile completed |
| **Win back quiet customers.** An offer they can use right away, such as 10% off or free delivery. | Segment: no order in 60 days | Session start | Full screen | Order placed within 7 days |
| **Stamp campaigns.** Show their stamps, the voucher they will unlock, and the time left. | Segment: one order from the reward | Session start | Modal | Order placed |
| **Turn on notifications.** Explain the value first, then ask for push permission. | Registered customers | Session start, once per customer | Modal with a **Request push permission** button | None |

<Tip>
Start with one campaign and add a control group. The results show you what works.
</Tip>

## Configuring an In-App Messaging Campaign

To set up an In-App Messaging campaign from scratch in Gameball, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to Dashboard**: Navigate to the **Gameball Dashboard**.
2. **Open Campaigns**: Click on **Campaigns**, then **Communication**.
3. **Create a Campaign**: Click **Create Communication Campaign**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-01-communication-campaigns.png" alt="Communication Campaigns page with the Communication menu and the Create Communication Campaign button highlighted" />
</Frame>

4. **Select In-App**: At the top of the **Communication** window, choose **In-App**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-02-create-in-app.png" alt="The Communication window with the In-App option highlighted" />
</Frame>

You build the campaign in four steps: **Rules**, **Message**, **Delivery**, and **Review**. Click **Save and close** at any step to finish later.

---

### Step 1: Rules

1. **Name Your Campaign**: Enter a **Campaign name**. Only your team sees it. It never appears to customers.

2. **Set Your Audience**: In the **Audience** section, choose who is eligible for this message:
   - **All Customers**
   - **Guest Customers**
   - **Registered Customers**
   - **Specific Customers**

   Gameball shows how many customers are in your audience right now.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-03-rules-name-audience.png" alt="Campaign name and Audience section in the Rules step" />
</Frame>

3. **Pick a Trigger**: In the **Triggers** section, choose when the message shows:
   - **Session start**: Shows the message when the customer opens the app.
   - **Action based**: Shows the message when the customer does something in your app. Choose the event, such as `place_order`. To narrow it down, click **Add Filter** and set a condition on the event's details, for example **total_price** **Greater than** **100**.

   Filters support **Is**, **Is not**, **Greater than**, **Greater than or equal**, **Less than**, **Less than or equal**, and **Between**.

<Note>
An action-based trigger only works with events your app sends through the Gameball SDK.
</Note>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-04-rules-trigger-action-based.png" alt="Action based trigger with the place_order event and a total_price filter" />
</Frame>

4. **Choose the Platforms**: Under **Platforms**, select **iOS**, **Android**, or both.

5. **Set When It Runs**:
   - **Starts**: Choose **As soon as it is live**, or **On a specific date**.
   - **Ends**: The campaign has no end date by default. Turn on the toggle to set one.

<Note>
Start and end times are evaluated on the device, in each customer's own time zone.
</Note>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-05-rules-platforms-schedule.png" alt="Platforms and When it runs sections" />
</Frame>

6. **Save and Continue**: Click **Save and continue**.

The **Who sees it, and when** panel on the right summarizes your choices as you go.

---

### Step 2: Message

1. **Choose a Message Type**: Pick **Slide-up**, **Modal**, or **Full screen**. You can change it later.

2. **Write the Content**: Write on the left and check the live preview on the right. Switch the preview between **iOS** and **Android**.
   - **Languages**: Use the language tabs, such as **EN** and **AR**, to write the message in each language you support. A tab with a **!** still needs content.
   - **Layout**: Choose **Text and image**, or **Image only** when your image is the whole message.
   - **Header**: Keep it short and specific.
   - **Message**: Up to 120 characters.

3. **Personalize the Message**: Insert the customer's own details with these variables: `{player_name}`, `{first_name}`, `{player_last_name}`, `{player_display_name}`, `{player_unique_id}`, `{player_email}`, `{points_balance}`, `{available_points}`, `{pending_points}`.

4. **Add an Image**: Upload a PNG, JPEG, JPG, or GIF, up to 5 MB. In a slide-up or modal, the image shows at the top of the message.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-07-message-editor.png" alt="The message editor with content on the left and the live preview on the right" />
</Frame>

5. **Add Buttons**: Click **Add button** to add up to two buttons. Labels are set per language. What a button does is shared across all languages. Each button also has its own background, text, and border colors.

   | On click | What happens |
   |---|---|
   | **No action** | Nothing happens. This is the default. |
   | **Close message** | Closes the message. |
   | **Open a web URL** | Opens a web page. |
   | **Deep link into the app** | Opens a screen in your app, such as `/rewards`. You can add arguments, one per line, as `key: value`. |
   | **Request push permission** | Asks the customer to allow push notifications. |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-08-message-button-actions.png" alt="Button settings with the On click options open" />
</Frame>

6. **Set the Colors and Tap Behavior**:
   - Under **Message colors**, set the **Background**, **Header**, **Text**, **Close button**, and **Overlay** colors.
   - Under **Behaviour**, use **When tapped** to choose what happens when the customer taps the message itself, outside the buttons.

   The preview is approximate. The real message follows the device theme.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-09-message-colors-behaviour.png" alt="Message colors and Behaviour sections" />
</Frame>

7. **Click Done**: Click **Done** to save the variant. To test the variant on your own phone first, click **Send to device**.

#### Full Screen Messages

A full screen message has a few extra settings:

- **Media**: The image fills the screen behind your text. An animated GIF plays as uploaded.
- **Show safe zone**: Turn it on under the preview to see the guides. Keep key content inside them so notches and home bars do not cover it.
- **Orientation**: Under **Display**, choose the orientation the message is shown in. If the device is held the other way, the message waits for the next opportunity instead of being skipped.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-10-message-full-screen.png" alt="A full screen variant with Show safe zone turned on in the preview" />
</Frame>

#### Variants and Control Group

Test more than one version to see which performs better.

- **Add variant**: Click **Add variant** and build another version, for example a full screen to compare with your modal.
- **Split the audience**: Set each variant's **Audience share**. The shares must add up to 100%. Click **Distribute evenly** to split them equally.
- **Add control group**: Click **Add control group**. These customers are eligible but see nothing. Comparing against them shows whether the campaign made the difference, and not something else, like a seasonal spike.

You can rename a variant, change its share, or change its message type from the **Variant settings** tab. Changing the type keeps your text but clears fields the new type does not use.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-11-message-variants-control-group.png" alt="Message variants with two variants at 45% each and a 10% control group" />
</Frame>

Click **Save and continue**.

---

### Step 3: Delivery

1. **Set How Often It Shows**: Choose **Once, ever**, or **Again after a set gap** and set the days between showings. You can also turn on:
   - **Also cap how often this campaign can show**: For example, no more than once per day. This cap applies to this campaign only.
   - **Stop after a total number of views**

   With both turned off, the message keeps showing until the end date, or until you stop it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-12-delivery-frequency.png" alt="How often it shows, with a 7-day gap and a daily cap" />
</Frame>

2. **Measure a Conversion Goal**: Turn on **Measure a conversion goal**.
   - Choose the **Event that counts as a conversion**, such as `place_order`.
   - Set **Count conversions within**, such as 7 days. A conversion only counts if it happens inside this window after the message is shown.
   - Each conversion is credited to the campaign and to the variant the customer saw.
   - To stop showing the campaign to customers who convert, turn on **Stop showing after conversion**. This applies for good, whatever the repeat settings above.

   Without a goal, the campaign's conversion numbers stay at zero.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-13-delivery-conversion-goal.png" alt="Conversion goal with place_order counted within 7 days" />
</Frame>

3. **Set the Priority**: Under **If more than one message is eligible at once**, choose **High**, **Normal**, or **Low**. Only the highest-priority eligible message is shown.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-14-delivery-priority.png" alt="Priority setting and the What limits delivery panel" />
</Frame>

4. **Save and Continue**: Click **Save and continue**.

The **What limits delivery** panel on the right lists everything that can hold this message back.

---

### Step 4: Review

1. **Check Before You Launch**: The **Before you launch** list shows anything that needs attention. For example, if a variant is missing a language, that share of your audience sees the default language instead. Click **Add it** to fix it.
2. **Review What They Will See**: Check the preview of every variant and the control group, then check the **Summary**.
3. **Send a Test Message**: Click **Send test message** and check it on your phone. The test happens on the device, so the dashboard cannot confirm it from here.
4. **Set Live**: Click **Set live**. To finish later, click **Save as draft**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-15-review.png" alt="Review step with Before you launch and What they will see" />
</Frame>

## Account-Level In-App Settings

These rules apply to every in-app campaign on your account.

1. On the **Communication Campaigns** page, click **Settings**.
2. Next to **In-app messages**, click **Edit**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-16-settings-menu.png" alt="The Settings menu with In-app messages highlighted" />
</Frame>

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Quiet hours** | Turn on **Pause in-app messages during quiet hours** and set **From** and **Until**, for example 22:00 to 08:00. No in-app message shows inside this window. Times use the customer's own device clock, not your account timezone. |
| **Time between messages** | The shortest gap allowed between any two in-app messages shown to the same customer. The default is 30 seconds. |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-docs/communication-campaigns/in-app-17-settings-in-app.png" alt="In-app messages settings with Quiet hours and Time between messages" />
</Frame>

Click **Save**.

## Tracking Performance

The **Communication Campaigns** list shows each campaign's **Trigger**, **Channel**, **Audience**, **State**, **Sent**, **Clicked**, and **Goal**. In-app campaigns show **In-App** in the **Channel** column.

<Note>
Statistics appear 10 to 30 minutes after customers interact with a message.
</Note>

## Managing Campaigns

- Find your campaigns under the **Live** and **Draft** tabs on the **Communication Campaigns** page.
- Click the options menu next to a campaign to **Edit**, **Duplicate**, or **Delete** it.
- Edits to a live campaign reach customers the next time they open your app.

## Important Notes

<Note>
**One message at a time.** Only one in-app message shows at a time. If two campaigns match the same moment, the higher-priority one shows.
</Note>

<Warning>
**Variants lock after launch.** Once a customer is assigned to a variant, the variant set and the audience split are frozen. Dates and caps can be changed but not removed. Everything else stays editable. Plan your test before you set the campaign live.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**Caps may be exceeded slightly.** Caps and total view limits are applied from reported statistics, which can lag by 10 to 30 minutes. A very active customer may see a capped message once more than expected inside that window.
</Warning>

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is my campaign not showing in the app?">
    Check that the campaign is live, that it targets the customer's platform, and that the customer is in the audience. Then check whether they already saw it, whether quiet hours or the time between messages is holding it back, and whether a higher-priority message showed instead. If nothing explains it, ask your development team to confirm that in-app messaging is turned on in the app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do customers who don't open the app get the message?">
    No. In-app messages only show inside your app. To reach customers outside the app, use a push notification campaign.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can two in-app messages show at the same time?">
    No. One message shows at a time, and priority decides which one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which language does a customer see?">
    The customer's preferred language. If a variant is missing that language, they see your default language.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do test messages affect my statistics?">
    No. Test messages show normally on the device but are not counted in your campaign results.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
