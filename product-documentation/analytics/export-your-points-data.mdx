---
title: "How Do I Track Points & Rewards Analytics?"
description: "Understand the Points & Rewards analytics page — what every metric means and how to export your raw data."
---

The **Points & Rewards** analytics page gives you a full picture of how points flow through your loyalty program: what's being earned, where it's coming from, how it's being spent, and how much is sitting unused. Use it to spot redemption gaps, track liability, and reconcile your finance reports.

---

## How to Get There

From the left-side menu, click **Analytics** → **Points & Rewards**.

---

## Filters

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Date range** (top left) | Scopes all time-series charts and totals to that period. |
| **Choose Tag** (top right) | Filters all data to customers who have a specific tag. |
| **Export** | Downloads all data on this page as a CSV file. |

---

## Liability

<Note>
Liability metrics show the **current totals** across your entire customer base and are not affected by the selected date range.
</Note>

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Total available points** | The combined sum of active and pending points across all customers — the full theoretical liability. |
| **Total pending points** | Points earned but not yet active, either waiting in a return window or pending redemption confirmation. |
| **Total active points** | Points customers can spend right now. |

---

## Accumulated Points

Points that were added to customer balances within the selected date range.

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Total accumulated points** | All points credited to customers in the period, regardless of source. |
| **Points source** | A chart breaking down accumulated points by where they came from (e.g. Cashback, Achievements, Referrals, Manual, Campaigns). The chart can be viewed weekly or at other intervals. |

---

## Deducted Points

Points that left customer balances within the selected date range.

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Total deducted points** | All points removed from balances in the period — includes redemptions, expirations, cancellations, and manual deductions. |
| **Deduction types** | Chart showing how points were removed, broken down by type (redeemed, expired, cancelled, manually deducted, etc.). |
| **Redemption by reward type** | What customers redeemed their points for — coupon, cashback, free product, etc. |
| **Expired Points by Source** | Expired points split by the source that originally issued them (e.g. Cashback, Campaigns). Helps you spot which earning sources produce the most waste. |
| **Redeemed Points by Source** | Redeemed points split by the source that originally issued them. Helps you understand which earning sources drive the most actual spending. |
| **Redemption rate** | The ratio of redeemed points to accumulated points, shown as a percentage. The higher this is, the more your rewards are driving real customer action. |

---

## Exporting Your Points Data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Points & Rewards">
    Go to **Analytics** → **Points & Rewards**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters (optional)">
    Filter by date range or tag to narrow down the export.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export">
    Click **Export** at the top right of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the CSV">
    Your data downloads as a CSV file with one row per day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Exported Fields

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `date` | The date for which the points data is reported |
| `earned_points` | Total points earned by customers on that date |
| `redeemed_points` | Points used/redeemed for rewards |
| `burned_points` | Points lost due to expiry, cancellation, partial refund, or manual deduction |
| `cashback_reward_points` | Points earned specifically from purchase/cashback rewards |
| `refunded_points` | Points returned to customers after a refund |
| `redeemed_fixed_points` | Points used directly on an order without coupon creation |
| `expired_points` | Points that expired before being redeemed |
| `cancelled_points` | Points cancelled due to order cancellation or reversal |
| `migrated_points` | Points transferred from a previous system or imported manually |
| `manual_added_points` | Points manually granted by admins |
| `manual_negative_points` | Points manually deducted by admins |
| `partially_refunded_points` | Points adjusted due to partial refunds |
| `campaign_points` | Points earned from campaigns (challenges, engagement triggers, etc.) |
| `referral_points` | Points earned through referral activities |
| `level_points` | Points awarded through tier progress |
| `redemption_rate` | Ratio of redeemed to earned points, shown as a percentage |
