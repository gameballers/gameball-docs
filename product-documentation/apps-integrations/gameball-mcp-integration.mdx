**Gameball MCP** lets you connect your own AI agent, like Claude, to your Gameball account and run it by asking in plain words. Ask the agent to create a campaign, check a setting or change a tier, and the change happens in your Gameball account. It works on its own, without the dashboard chat.

---

## What Can My AI Agent Do?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Answer questions about your program data" icon="chart-line">
    Ask questions like "Why did redemption drop last week?" and get answers from your own account data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Create and manage reward campaigns" icon="gift">
    Create reward campaigns, then change their reward, schedule, audience or budget and turn them on or off. A new campaign is saved off until you turn it on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Create email, push and SMS campaigns" icon="envelope">
    Create communication campaigns, write the message and schedule it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Create segments and manage customer tags and points" icon="users">
    Create segments, such as customers who haven't bought in 60 days, and add or remove customer tags and points.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="View and change your program and account settings" icon="gear">
    View and change your tiers, cashback rate and redemption options. Manage account settings like events, custom attributes, branches, tags, languages and collections.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Important Notes

<Note>
**New Campaigns Are Saved Off**: A campaign your agent creates stays off until you turn it on.
</Note>

<Warning>
**Customer Data Stays Private**: An outside AI agent can't look up a customer's profile.
</Warning>

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need the dashboard chat to use Gameball MCP?">
    No. Gameball MCP works on its own, without the dashboard chat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will my agent turn on a campaign it creates?">
    No. A new campaign is saved off until you turn it on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my agent look up a customer's profile?">
    No. To keep customer data private, an outside AI agent can't look up a customer's profile. It can still add or remove customer tags and points.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
