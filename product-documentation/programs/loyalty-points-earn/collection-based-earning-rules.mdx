Customize your cashback system on Gameball to offer different cashback values for each collection in your store. This flexibility allows you to incentivize purchases from specific collections and enhance customer engagement.

On the **Points for Orders** page, you give a collection its own rate with a **special rate**, and you stop a collection from earning with an **exception**. The old **Collection-based Earning Rule** setting and its **Collection Rules** tab are gone. Your existing collection rules are listed under **Special Rates** and keep earning as before.

## Prerequisites

- Ensure that you have activated the **Cashback** feature on your Gameball account.
- Confirm that your store collections are properly set up.

---

## What Replaced Each Old Option

| Old option | Now |
|---|---|
| **All Products** | Your **Base Rate**. Every product earns it unless an exception or a special rate applies. |
| **All Products Except Some Collections** | **Settings › Exceptions**, with a **Collection** condition. |
| **Custom Cashback** (Collection Rules) | **Add Special Rate**, with a **Collection** condition. |
| **Selected Collections** (only these earn) | Can't be created any more. Existing setups keep earning. When you edit one, Gameball asks you to switch to exceptions. To get the same result, set your base rate to 0 and add a special rate for the collections that should earn. |

## Give a Collection Its Own Rate

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Points for Orders">
Go to **Programs > Earn Points** in your Gameball dashboard. The **Points for Orders** page opens.
</Step>

<Step title="Add a Special Rate">
In the **Special Rates** card, click **Add Special Rate**.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose the Collections">
Click **Add Condition**, choose **Collection** under **Products**, and pick the collections. Only the matching products earn the special rate.
</Step>

<Step title="Set the Rate">
Set the rate in **Points per [currency]** or **% back**. **Everyone** earns the rate you set. Click **Add a Tier** to give a tier its own rate for the same collections.

<Note>
**Fixed Cashback Value** (a set number of points per product, whatever its price) can't be chosen for new rates. Existing fixed rates keep earning.
</Note>
</Step>

<Step title="Review and Save">
Enter a **Rate name**, check the summary, then click **Save**. Products from the chosen collections now earn the special rate instead of the base and tier rates.
</Step>
</Steps>

[See the full Special Rates walkthrough](/product-documentation/programs/loyalty-points-earn/launch-and-configure-your-earn-pointing-system#special-rates)

## Stop a Collection from Earning Points

1. On the **Points for Orders** page, click **Settings**.
2. Click **Edit** next to **Exceptions**.
3. Click **Add Condition**, choose **Collection**, and pick the collections that shouldn't earn points.
4. Click **Save**.

[Learn more about exceptions](/product-documentation/programs/loyalty-points-earn/eligible-orders-for-earning)

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Can I reward customers on higher tiers with different cashback values under the same collection?" icon="crown">
Yes. In the special rate's **Who and How Much** step, click **Add a Tier**, choose the tier, and set its rate.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if an item belongs to multiple collections with different cashback rules?" icon="layer-group">
Gameball will reward based on the **highest cashback point value**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if multiple different items from the same collection are purchased?" icon="bag-shopping">
The points reward applies per item.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
