Collect customer feedback directly through Gameball and pay for it in points. **Survey** is a reward campaign type with its own question builder, an optional completion reward, and three ways to reach customers: inside the loyalty widget, as a storefront popup, or through a standalone shareable link.

<Note>
This is a native Gameball campaign type. For the Shopify-Flow-based template that rewards customers for completing a survey on a separate app (e.g. Okendo), see [Fill a Survey (Shopify Flow)](/product-documentation/rewards-campaigns/templates/engagement-gamification/fill-a-survey).
</Note>

## How to Set Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Gameball">
    Log in to your Gameball dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Reward Campaign">
    Click **Add Reward Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Template">
    Choose the **Survey** template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Campaign">
    Edit the campaign's setup to match your goals (see configuration sections below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Click **Set Live** to activate the campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Campaign Configuration

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Audience" icon="users">
  - **All Customers**
  - **Anonymous Customers (Not logged in)**
  - **Specific Customers:** Filter by segment, tag, or customer attributes to limit who can participate.

  As you adjust the audience, Gameball shows how many customers are currently eligible to participate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Triggers" icon="bolt">
  - **Customer completes the survey:** The reward is issued once the customer submits all required questions.
  - **Web Popup (URL-based trigger):** Show the survey as a popup on specific storefront pages.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Repeatability" icon="repeat">
  Choose how many times a customer can earn this campaign:

  - **Unlimited**
  - **Limited to X times overall**
  - **Time-based limit (per period)**
  - **Combined** (overall + per period)

  The completion reward is issued only once per customer per allowed repetition, no matter which channel (widget, popup, or shareable link) they used.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Info>
This template uses the standard reward campaign setup. See the [Generic Setup Guide](/product-documentation/rewards-campaigns/start-from-scratch/setup-reward-campaign) for details.
</Info>

## Question Builder

Build your survey from seven supported question types:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Single Choice** | Customer picks one option from a list. |
| **Multiple Choice** | Customer can select more than one option. |
| **Short Text** | A single-line free-text answer. |
| **Long Text** | A multi-line free-text answer. |
| **Rating Scale** | Customer rates on a scale of 1–5 or 1–10. |
| **NPS** | Net Promoter Score question, scored 0–10. |
| **Image Choice** | Customer picks one option from a set of images. |

Add, reorder, and delete questions freely, and mark any question **Required** or **Optional**.

<Frame>
  ![Survey completion reward, no reward option, dates and limits](/images/product-docs/rewards-campaigns/templates/engagement-gamification/survey-campaign-questions-overview.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
Reward the survey with points or a coupon from your existing redemption rules — or run it with no reward at all. You can also set a start/end date and a total participation limit for the whole campaign, independent of per-customer repeatability.
</Note>

## Distribution

Reach customers through three channels, configurable independently:

- **Gameball Widget:** The survey appears inside the loyalty widget.
- **Storefront Popup:** Enable popup mode with a configurable trigger — page load, time delay, or exit intent.
- **Shareable Link:** Copy an auto-generated link that renders the survey on a Gameball-hosted page, no widget required. Useful for emails, SMS, or social posts.

## Customization

Customize branding and copy for each stage of the survey — Intro, Question, and Success screens.

<Frame>
  ![Survey question customization with branding and live preview](/images/product-docs/rewards-campaigns/templates/engagement-gamification/survey-campaign-question-customization.png)
</Frame>

- **Branding:** Set the main color and button text color.
- **Content:** Edit button labels and other on-screen copy per screen, with support for both English and Arabic.
- Question transitions and selections are animated, with a confirmation screen shown on submission.

## Customer Experience & Data

- **Identified customers** are credited automatically for their responses.
- **Anonymous link visitors** are handled based on your campaign configuration.
- Any survey answer can be stored back on the customer as a **customer attribute**, so responses become segmentation and targeting criteria for future campaigns, automations, and audiences.
- Responses are stored per campaign and exportable as CSV.
- Aggregated results for every published survey are available in **Insights**. See [Survey & Quiz Response Analytics](/product-documentation/rewards-campaigns/reward-campaigns-insights#survey-quiz-response-analytics).

---

## Important Notes

<Note>
**Customer Eligibility**

The survey updates dynamically based on customer eligibility, ensuring customers only see surveys they can still participate in. If a customer no longer belongs to the campaign's target segment, the survey is automatically removed from the widget and won't reappear unless eligibility is restored.
</Note>
