## Single Currency Setup

By default, Gameball operates in one currency. You set that currency once and it applies everywhere: earn and redeem programs, the widget, analytics, and exports.

To configure your currency:

1. Go to **Settings > Admin Settings > General**.
2. Scroll to the **Currency** section.
3. Choose your currency and whether to display it as a symbol (e.g. `$`) or a code (e.g. `USD`).
4. Click **Save**.

The chosen currency applies to all money fields across the dashboard and the Gameball widget.

## Multi-Currency Support

If you sell to customers in more than one country, you can enable Multi-Currency so each shopper sees loyalty values — points equivalence, coupon amounts — in their own currency. Your rules, analytics, and liability always stay in your base currency.

### Enabling Multi-Currency

1. Go to **Settings > Admin Settings > General**.
2. Scroll to the **Currency** section and turn on **Multi-currency**.
3. Set your **Base currency** — this is the currency all internal calculations use.
4. Click **Add currency** for each additional currency you accept, and enter your exchange rate as: `1 [base] = X [other currency]`.
5. Click **Save**.

<Note>
You can edit exchange rates at any time. New rates apply from that point forward — past transactions keep the rate they were recorded with.
</Note>

### How Currency Conversion Works

Once Multi-Currency is enabled:

- **Orders and APIs:** When an order or API call includes a currency, Gameball converts the amount to your base currency using your rate and stores the original currency, the rate used, and the base amount. Order details and Recent Events show the conversion, for example: `1,000 AED @ 1.02 = 1,020 SAR`.
- **Widget and mobile SDKs:** Points value and coupon amounts are shown in the shopper's currency automatically.
- **Dashboard:** Every money input shows its equivalent in each enabled currency so your team always knows the base value.
- **Salla stores:** Gameball follows the currency the shopper selected on the storefront.
- **Exports and data warehouse:** Transaction exports include new columns for currency, exchange rate, and base amount.

### Targeting Customers by Currency

Two new customer attributes are available for use in segments, reward campaigns, communication campaigns, and automations:

| Attribute | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Last order currency** | The currency used on the customer's most recent order |
| **Current currency** | The currency the customer is currently browsing in |

Learn how to use these in segments in [How to Segment Customers](/product-documentation/customer-segmentation/how-to-segment-customers).
