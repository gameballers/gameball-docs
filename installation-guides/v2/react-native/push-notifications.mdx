Gameball uses Firebase to deliver maximum experience in your app.

## Configure Gameball With Your Firebase

Before you start, you must configure your Firebase on your Gameball account. Follow the steps in [Configure your Firebase account on Gameball for mobile push notifications](/product-documentation/admin-settings/configure-firebase-push-notifications) article from our Help Center related to push notifications.
