title: "Customer Profile Updates"
description: "This webhook is triggered every time there's an update to a customer's profile, ensuring your app stays in sync with the latest changes."
---


**Common Use Cases:**

* **Sync Customer Points and VIP Tier:** Keep your CRM system up-to-date with the latest customer points and VIP tier information.
* **Track Profile Changes:** Automatically update other systems or services when customer profile details change.
* **Track Program Membership:** Know when a customer is excluded from or included back into your loyalty program, or deleted.
* **Track Referrals:** See who referred a customer and how many customers they have referred.

This webhook ensures that your systems reflect the most accurate and up-to-date customer information.

## Customer Profile Webhook POST Data

```json
{
  "event": "customer.profile.updated",
  "client_id": "2155",
  "customer_id": "webhook-test",
  "gb_customer_id": 1234,
  "created_at": "2024-11-19T17:34:42.297Z",
  "data": {
    "email": "customer@example.com",
    "mobile_number": "+XX-XXXX-XXXX",
    "tier_name": "basic",
    "tier_order": 1,
    "referral_code": "referral_123",
    "referral_link": "www.example.com",
    "activated_points": 90,
    "pending_points": 10,
    "total_points": 100,
    "status": "included",
    "is_deleted": false,
    "referred_by_customer_id": "referrer_123",
    "is_referral_activated": true,
    "total_referrals": 3,
    "active_referrals": 2,
    "pending_referrals": 1
  }
}
```

## Webhook Fields

<ResponseField name="event" type="string" required>
This indicates that the event triggering the webhook is related to a customer profile update
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="client_id" type="string" required>
The unique identifier for the client (your app) that's receiving the webhook.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="customer_id" type="string" required>
Unique identifier for the customer that you can reference across the customer's whole lifetime. Could be a database ID, random string, email, or anything that uniquely identifies the customer.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="gb_customer_id" type="number" required>
The Gameball internal identifier for the customer.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="string" required>
The timestamp when the webhook event was created, formatted in ISO 8601.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data" type="object" required>
This contains the updated customer profile data.

<Expandable title="Customer Profile Object">

<ResponseField name="email" type="string">
The customer's email address.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="mobile_number" type="string">
The customer's mobile number.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="tier_name" type="string">
The customer's current loyalty tier name.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="tier_order" type="number">
The order/level of the customer's current loyalty tier.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="referral_code" type="string">
A referral code associated with the customer.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="referral_link" type="string">
A referral link for the customer.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="activated_points" type="number">
The points the customer has currently activated and can use.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="pending_points" type="number">
Points that are pending and will be activated later.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="total_points" type="number">
The customer's total accumulated points, including both activated and pending points.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
Whether the customer is part of your loyalty program. Possible values:

* `included`: the customer is in the program and can earn and redeem.
* `excluded`: the customer has been excluded from the program.

This matches the [Update Customer Activation](/api-reference/customers/management/update-customer-activation) API: `included` is `isActive: true` and `excluded` is `isActive: false`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="is_deleted" type="boolean">
`true` only on the final webhook sent when the customer is deleted. `false` on every other webhook.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="referred_by_customer_id" type="string">
The `customer_id` of the customer who referred this customer.

* `null`: the customer was not referred.
* `deleted_customer`: the customer was referred, but the referrer has since been deleted.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="is_referral_activated" type="boolean">
Whether this customer's own referral has been activated. `false` if the customer was not referred.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="total_referrals" type="number">
The number of customers this customer has referred. This is the sum of `active_referrals` and `pending_referrals`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="active_referrals" type="number">
The number of referred customers whose referral has been activated.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="pending_referrals" type="number">
The number of referred customers whose referral has not been activated yet.
</ResponseField>

</Expandable>

</ResponseField>

## Customer Deletion

When a customer is deleted, one final webhook is sent for that customer with `is_deleted` set to `true`. The other fields carry the customer's last known values. No further webhooks are sent for that customer.

## Notes

* **Referral counts update on the referrer's next webhook.** When a referred customer signs up or their referral is activated, the referrer's counts change, but that alone does not trigger a webhook for the referrer. The new counts appear the next time the referrer's profile is updated.
* **Referral counts can be `null`.** In rare cases `total_referrals`, `active_referrals`, and `pending_referrals` are `null` when they could not be loaded for that webhook. Treat `null` as unknown, not as zero.

<Note>
The fields `status`, `is_deleted`, `referred_by_customer_id`, `is_referral_activated`, `total_referrals`, `active_referrals`, and `pending_referrals` are only available on **V4** webhooks. Webhooks on earlier versions keep their existing payload and do not receive a webhook when a customer is deleted.
</Note>
